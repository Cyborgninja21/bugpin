# Changelog v1.2.5

### Fixes

- Standardized Admin Console scrollbars with theme-aware scroll areas across pages, the sidebar, dialogs, tables, and lists.
- Styled native scrollbars in text editors, email previews, and the reporting widget to match light and dark themes, removing bright scrollbar tracks in dark mode.
- Preserved dialog field autofocus and avoided extra tab stops in forms and tables while keeping overflowing read-only content keyboard accessible.
- Hidden custom scrollbars when the sidebar is collapsed to icons.
